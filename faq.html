How to use the shelf.

Everything SkillShelf can do — finding, installing, saving, stacking and sharing resources.

Finding things

How do I find a skill?+

Type in the search bar — it matches names, descriptions, tags and even install commands. Press / anywhere (or Ctrl/⌘ + K) to jump straight to it, and hit the red × to clear.

What do the category pills do?+

They filter the shelf — AI Skills, UI & Design, Libraries, MCP & Tools. The number on each pill is its live count.

How does sorting work?+

The Sort dropdown (right side of the filters) offers Curated (default order), Copyable first (installable skills on top) and A–Z. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

What happens when I click a tag?+

The whole shelf filters by that tag and scrolls you to the results. Clicking a tag inside the detail view does the same thing.

Installing

How do I install a single skill?+

Hit Copy command on its card, paste into your terminal and run it. Commands look like npx skills add owner/repo --skill skill-name. Cards without a command copy the official link instead.

What does an install command actually do?+

It downloads that skill package into your AI coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode and others). As always: review a command and its source repo before running anything on your machine.

What is the detail view?+

Click any card title to open it. You get install variants (project, global with -g -y, and entire repo), clickable tags, related skills and the official page link. Esc closes it.

How do I install several skills at once?+

Tap + on every card you want. A bar appears at the bottom with your count: Copy all commands copies every install command at once (link-only entries are skipped and counted), Copy as Markdown makes a checklist you can paste straight to your agent, and Clear empties the stack. Your stack survives reloads.

Saving & sharing

Where do saved items live?+

Tap the bookmark on any card and it lands on your Saved page. Everything is stored in your own browser — no account, no uploads. See the privacy notes.

Can I share my shelf or move it to another device?+

Yes — on the Saved page use Copy link for a shareable URL or Export for a JSON file, then Import it elsewhere. Unknown entries are skipped and duplicates are never added twice.

What are the ?cat= and ?q= links?+

Shareable shortcuts to a pre-filtered shelf — the footer uses them, e.g. index.html?cat=Libraries opens the shelf already filtered to libraries.

Around the site

What keyboard shortcuts exist?+

/ or Ctrl/⌘ + K focuses search, Esc closes the detail view or blurs the search field.

What is the ring button in the corner?+

Back-to-top with a scroll progress ring — the stroke fills as you read down the page. Click it to glide back up.

Does the newsletter sign me up for emails?+

No — it's a demo placeholder. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere, consistent with the no-tracking policy.
