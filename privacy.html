privacy in plain words

Your shelf stays on your device.

No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. Here's exactly what this site stores — and what it doesn't.

What we collect: nothing

SkillShelf has no server, no database and no user accounts. There is nowhere for your data to be sent, and nothing is tracked about your visits.

What your browser stores locally

To remember your preferences on your own device, the site uses your browser's local storage — never cookies, and never sent anywhere:

  • Saved resources and your install stack.
  • Your sort preference and light/dark theme.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it permanently.

Third-party requests

Resource logos are loaded from Google's favicon service, and fonts come from Google Fonts. Those providers receive a standard web request (including your IP address) when those assets load, governed by their own privacy policies. Opening any listed resource takes you to that owner's site, which has its own practices.

No cookie banner — on purpose

Cookie consent banners exist for sites that track you. Since this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, there is nothing to consent to. If analytics is ever added, this page will be updated first.
